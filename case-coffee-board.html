Case study, Coffee Board of India at ICT Infracon

A coffee shop for customers and a land-survey tool for growers.

Two modules I built for the Coffee Board of India: one sells coffee online, the other lets growers register their land for subsidies.

My role
Built both modules
Stack
Java, Spring Boot, Angular, MSSQL
Integrations
SBI payment gateway, India Post

The eShop

The Coffee Board wanted to sell coffee directly to people who love it. I built the eShop module where customers order and pay online.

  • Payment: integrated with the SBI payment gateway.
  • Delivery: integrated with India Post, so orders reach customers by post.
  • Labels: dispatchers download the shipping label for each order straight from the app.

The polygon module

Coffee growers can register for subsidies, and that starts with their land. I built the polygon module, where growers add their land survey to the app and register.

Around both modules

  • An OTP-based registration and verification module.
  • Authentication and authorization with Spring Security.
  • Support for the production deployment.
