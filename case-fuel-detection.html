Case study, VZone at Gnapi Technologies

Catching refuels and fuel theft in a noisy sensor stream.

Flink-based detection of refuels and drains, built to stay correct when the same message arrives twice.

My role
Flink job and its writes
Stack
Flink (Java), Kafka
Also built
Fuel estimation for vehicles without sensors

The problem

Fleet operators want to know two things about fuel: when a tank was filled, and when fuel went missing. A drain while a vehicle is parked can mean theft.

The difficulty is that a fuel reading moves for innocent reasons too. The engine burns fuel, the vehicle parks on a slope, the tank sloshes. Detection has to pick out the real jumps and leave the noise alone.

What I built

  • A Flink job that detects refuels and drains from the stream of vehicle events.
  • Idempotent upserts for its output, so results stay correct under Kafka's at-least-once delivery.
  • Fuel estimation for vehicles that have no fuel sensor.

Getting duplicates right

Kafka delivers each message at least once. After a retry or a restart, the same message can arrive a second time. If every arrival inserted a new row, one refuel would be reported twice.

I made the writes idempotent upserts. The same event always lands on the same row, so delivering it again changes nothing.

Around it

I also built VZone's Notification Center, which delivers alerts in the app and as web push through FCM.
